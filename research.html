Building real BCI systems.
We build applied brain-computer interface systems that translate neural signals into real control. Our current focus is an EEG-powered wheelchair add-on that converts motor imagery into wheelchair movement.

EEG-Controlled Wheelchair via Motor Imagery
An electric wheelchair add-on that translates motor imagery into joystick commands, enabling hands-free navigation. The system classifies three motor imagery states: imagined foot movement (forward), right hand (rotate), and rest.
