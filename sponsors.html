We build at the intersection of neuroscience and engineering.

NeuroTech@VT is a student-led research and engineering club at Virginia Tech focused on brain-computer interfaces, neural signal processing, and open-source neurotechnology. We design hardware, write firmware, and publish our work — training the next generation of BCI engineers through hands-on projects and collaboration with faculty labs.

Active Projects
Mind-Controlled Wheelchair

A non-invasive BCI system that translates EEG motor imagery signals into real-time wheelchair navigation commands. The system uses a dry-electrode headset, custom signal processing pipeline, and embedded control firmware to enable hands-free mobility for individuals with motor impairments.
