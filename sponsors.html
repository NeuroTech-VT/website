Active Projects
Mind-Controlled Wheelchair

Electric wheelchairs are steered with a joystick, which rules them out for people with limited hand control. We are building an add-on that mounts over a wheelchair joystick and drives it using signals read from a non-invasive EEG headset. This add-on approach allows us to create a solution that'll be widely applicable to a wide range of electric wheelchairs instead of forcing the user to buy a new "BCI wheelchair".
